# Data Sources
==============================================


## 1. Gardiner_Sign_List.csv
   A structured listing of Gardiner sign codes mapped to
   their corresponding Unicode Egyptian Hieroglyph characters.
   Data was extracted programmatically via a custom script
   that parsed the source files of JSesh.

   Source: JSesh (https://jsesh.qenherkhopeshef.org)


## 2. Alan_Gardiners_List_of_Hieroglyphic_Signs.xlsx
   Alan Gardiner's original classification of Egyptian
   hieroglyphic signs, organized by category (A–Aa and Z).
   Each sign includes its Gardiner code, description,
   and phonetic value where applicable.

   Source: Gardiner, A. H. (1957). Egyptian Grammar: Being an
           Introduction to the Study of Hieroglyphs (3rd ed.).
           Griffith Institute, Oxford.
           https://github.com/omnika-datastore/alan-gardiner-list-of-hieroglyphic-signs


## 3. egyptian_word_dictionary.csv
   A word-level transliteration dictionary containing individual
   Egyptian words extracted from sentence-level data.
   Built for fine-tuning purposes.

   How it was built:
   Two datasets were merged:
     - BBAW Egyptian Dataset
       https://huggingface.co/datasets/phiwi/bbaw_egyptian
     - Thesaurus Linguae Aegyptiae (TLA) — Earlier Egyptian Original v18
       https://huggingface.co/datasets/thesaurus-linguae-aegyptiae/tla-Earlier_Egyptian_original-v18-premium

   The transliteration column from each dataset was split
   row by row to extract individual Word_Transliteration tokens,
   producing a clean word-level dictionary.
